# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete aiming to carb-load for a high-intensity 2-hour marathon training run:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3,200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (110g), Fat (80g), Carbs (430g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), sunflower seeds (20g), and honey (20g). Whole milk (240ml).
  * Protein: 14g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 85g

* **Pre-Run Snack (8:30 AM - 30 min before run):** White bread (60g) with jam (30g) and chia seeds (10g). Orange juice (250ml).
  * Protein: 6g, Fat: 3g, Carbs: 75g

* **Post-Run Snack (11:00 AM - within 30 min of finishing):** Chocolate milk (500ml) with a rice cake (35g) and banana (120g).
  * Protein: 18g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 95g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Lentil and vegetable soup (400ml) made with red lentils (80g dry), carrots (80g), celery (50g), and vegetable broth. Whole wheat bread (80g) with olive oil (10ml).
  * Protein: 20g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 85g

* **Afternoon Snack (4:00 PM):** Greek yogurt (200g, plain) with granola (40g), berries (100g), and honey (15g).
  * Protein: 18g, Fat: 6g, Carbs: 55g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Chickpea curry (chickpeas 150g cooked) with spinach (150g), tomatoes (100g), onions (60g), and coconut milk (100ml). Basmati rice (120g cooked).
  * Protein: 20g, Fat: 28g, Carbs: 80g

* **Evening Snack (9:00 PM):** Smoothie with plant-based protein powder (25g), mango (150g), and oat milk (240ml).
  * Protein: 14g, Fat: 15g, Carbs: 55g

This meal plan prioritizes easily digestible carbohydrates for glycogen loading, includes adequate plant-based protein sources, and strategically times carbs around the training session for optimal endurance performance while accommodating vegetarian and nut-free restrictions.
